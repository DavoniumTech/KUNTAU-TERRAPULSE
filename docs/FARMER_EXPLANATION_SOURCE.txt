WHAT THIS MAP SHOWS

This is a satellite radar measurement, not an ordinary camera photograph.

BEFORE: what the satellite measured on 8 October 2026.
AFTER: what it measured on 20 October 2026.
CHANGE: places where the measured radar return is higher or lower in the later observation.

A change does not automatically mean flooding, crop damage or better crop growth. Water, vegetation, soil surface, moisture and viewing conditions can all affect radar measurements.

WHAT TO DO: use highlighted locations as places to investigate. Check with farmers, field observations, rainfall records and local river information before making a decision.
