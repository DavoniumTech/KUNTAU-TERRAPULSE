# TERRAPULSE user guide — farmers, communities, students and science teams

KUNTAU TERRAPULSE helps people look at two satellite radar observations and ask better questions about a place. It does not replace local knowledge, river gauges, weather information or field inspection.

## For farmers and community members

1. Open **Learn & field guide → Farmer & community**.
2. Read **What am I looking at?** The map shows how strongly radar energy returned from the ground when the satellite passed. It is not a normal camera photograph.
3. Open **Compare** and choose Before, After, Swipe or Change.
4. Read the legend. The colours describe radar measurements. They are not danger levels and do not prove that a flood happened.
5. Use the highlighted location as a prompt for a local check. Ask what people observed, when and where. Check rain and river information when available.
6. Share field photographs or people's personal information only with their permission.

### Four words made simple

- **Radar:** invisible microwave energy sent from a satellite and measured when some returns.
- **Backscatter:** the radar signal that comes back from the ground.
- **Pixel:** one small square in a digital map, representing a measured area.
- **Field check:** checking the place or asking trusted local observers what happened.

## For students and children

1. Open **Learn & field guide → Students & children**.
2. Follow the radar echo diagram: send, listen, compare.
3. Complete the three-question knowledge check.
4. Remember: a radar image is a measurement image, not a phone photograph and not a forecast.

## For GIS and science teams

Open **Learn & field guide → Science team** and **Data & methods**. Review full product IDs, acquisition modes, CRS, pixel size, QA summaries and processing notes. The current comparison is exploratory because SHSH and DHDH modes differ and both QA summaries have an outstanding identification-group check failure.

## Map backgrounds and mobile data

The coordinate grid is selected by default and does not request map tiles. The optional satellite basemap uses an internet connection; provider attribution is shown in the map footer. The packaged NISAR preview layers are included locally.

## Guided presentation

Open **Learn & field guide → Start guided tour**. It walks through the project in five steps. For a video, use the computer or phone's built-in screen recorder while presenting the tour. The app does not autoplay or download a large video.

## Important limit

This demonstration does not measure river depth, water level, crop health or future flood probability. Always combine satellite evidence with independent observations and a field check.
