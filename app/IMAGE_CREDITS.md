## v0.7.0 additions

- The compact local Earth texture is included as `assets/earth-blue-marble.jpg`; it is decorative Earth imagery, not a Kura observation. Refer to NASA/GSFC Blue Marble Next Generation imagery for the public Earth-imagery source context: https://svs.gsfc.nasa.gov/3615/ .
- The 3D satellite is a stylised original KTP Three.js construction informed by NASA/JPL-Caltech NISAR artist-concept imagery. It is not an engineering model or live satellite track: https://science.nasa.gov/photojournal/nisar-in-orbit-artists-concept/ .
- The learning diagrams under `assets/education/` are original KTP vector illustrations. They are teaching aids, not photographs or remotely sensed evidence.
- The optional satellite basemap is Esri World Imagery. Attribution is displayed in the map footer when this basemap is selected. Offline coordinate grid remains the default.


## v0.8.2 interactive globe

The interactive Earth is a Three.js sphere rendered from the bundled `assets/earth-blue-marble.jpg` texture. The atmosphere, cloud wisps, star field and satellite/orbit are generated as interface visuals. The Kura marker is an approximate coordinate locator. They are not an active satellite position, NISAR acquisition footprint, live data stream or science layer.
