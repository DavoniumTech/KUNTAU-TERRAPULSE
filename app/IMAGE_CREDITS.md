# KUNTAU TERRAPULSE — image and visual credits

## Earth and orbital visuals
- `assets/earth-blue-marble.jpg` is bundled as decorative Earth imagery based on NASA/Goddard Blue Marble Next Generation visual material: https://svs.gsfc.nasa.gov/3615/ . It is not a NISAR acquisition or a Kura observation.
- `globe3d.js` renders the interactive Three.js Earth sphere. The atmosphere, cloud wisps, star field, conceptual orbit and satellite are interface visualizations, not live telemetry or an engineering orbit model.
- `assets/space-doodle-pattern.svg` is an original decorative line-art wallpaper for the application interface. Its planets, stars, rocket and satellite are illustrative icons.

## Learning and observation visuals
- Vector illustrations under `assets/education/` are original KUNTAU TERRAPULSE teaching aids. They are not photographs or remote-sensing evidence.
- Images under `data/kura/overlays/` and `data/kura/visualizations/` are derived display previews from the supplied NISAR evidence package. The difference preview is exploratory and must not be described as a validated flood or crop-damage map.

## Product identity
- The application icon combines the KTP product monogram with the KUNTAU Science Academy crest supplied for the project collaboration.
- Optional satellite basemap tiles are served by Esri World Imagery. Attribution is displayed in the map footer when that basemap is selected; the offline coordinate grid is the default.
