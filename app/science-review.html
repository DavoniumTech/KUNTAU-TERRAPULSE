IN PLAIN LANGUAGE

What does the satellite measure?

✦

A radar satellite sends microwave signals towards the Earth's surface and measures the energy that returns.

The return can be affected by the surface and by the way the satellite observes it. It is not an ordinary camera photograph and it is not a direct crop-health score.

A radar signal is evidence to investigate—not an automatic farming instruction.

SAR

Radar imaging that can observe the surface without relying on sunlight.

HH

A radar polarisation channel. It describes how the radar signal is transmitted and received.

HV

A different polarisation channel that can provide additional information about the scattering surface.

GCOV

A NISAR geocoded polarimetric covariance product. Its measurements and quality layers need careful interpretation.

Inspect valid-data masks, quality flags, NoData definitions, and number-of-looks information.

04
Scientific interpretation

Decide whether the common HH measurements are suitable for a limited comparison and record the limitations.

05
Independent evidence

Identify field observations or other relevant information before making agricultural interpretations.

Same map grid is not enough. Geometric alignment does not, by itself, establish radiometric or scientific comparability.

FOR FARMERS AND COMMUNITIES

What can we say today?

✓ We can say

We have two radar observations from different dates, and the team is checking whether a reliable comparison is possible.

× We cannot claim

  • These crops are healthy or damaged.
  • This field has flooded.
  • Soil moisture increased or decreased.
  • A farmer should irrigate, plant, or harvest.
“Radar measurements from these two dates look different in some places. Our team is checking whether the observations are suitable for a reliable comparison. This result is not a diagnosis of crop health, flood damage, or soil moisture.”
