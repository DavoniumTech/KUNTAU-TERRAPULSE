# KTP demonstration video storyboard (about 75–90 seconds)

The app includes a five-step guided tour. Record it using the device's built-in screen recorder. Do not autoplay a large video when the app opens; this protects mobile-data use and lets people choose when to watch.

| Time | Screen | Suggested narration |
|---|---|---|
| 0–12 s | Overview | “This is KUNTAU TERRAPULSE, a KUNTAU Science Academy Earth-observation workspace.” |
| 12–25 s | Explore map | “We begin with a small test area near Kura, Kano State. The rectangle is a test footprint, not a verified river boundary.” |
| 25–43 s | Compare | “Here are NISAR radar observations from 8 and 20 October 2026. The display compares the shared Frequency-A HH measurement.” |
| 43–59 s | Change layer and Insights | “The colours show places where the measured radar return differs. This is an exploratory signal, not proof of flooding or crop damage.” |
| 59–75 s | Learn & field guide | “The field guide explains the image for farmers and communities, students and science teams.” |
| 75–90 s | Data & methods | “The product identifiers, dates, quality notes and processing records make the evidence traceable. Local checks are still necessary.” |

## Recording notes

- Use landscape orientation for laptop/desktop presentation; use portrait for a phone walkthrough.
- Keep the optional online satellite basemap off unless needed; the offline coordinate grid is enough for the data explanation.
- Do not display login credentials, browser downloads folders or private files.
- Do not say KTP predicts floods, measures water depth or confirms crop damage.
- Include the outstanding SHSH/DHDH comparability and QA warning in the closing frame.
